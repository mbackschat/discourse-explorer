## Executive summary

Response times in user-facing categories (Help & Hints, Bug Reports, Translations & Localization) are measured in days, not hours, with medians between 111 and 160 hours. Three actionable unanswered topics sit in exactly those categories: a navigation help request in the Drowned Market, a softlock bug on Amiga targeting a specific quest handoff, and a localization gap for untranslated vocal stems in Crown of Brine II. Activity is heavily concentrated on two users — scurvy_harpooner dominates Translations & Localization and Speedruns & Challenges (50% and 42% of replies respectively), while salty_cabin-boy leads Show & Tell and is the top responder in Bug Reports. If either leaves, those categories lose their primary responder. The forum shows a clear founding burst in May 2025 followed by steady low-volume activity through the year, with a second peak in March 2026 — suggesting an active but small community that would benefit from explicit triage of its stale help queue.

### Drill-down: topic 20 — Drowned Market help request (Help & Hints)

User jolly_helmsman is stuck in the Drowned Market level, unable to progress past its shifting platforms and underwater passages. The post identifies two concrete friction points: (1) disorientation — the interconnected layout makes it easy to revisit areas without noticing, causing players to miss key items required to unlock doors; (2) changed audio cues in the remaster — the music that used to shift near secret paths is described as muted or different from the original, removing a navigation signal the player was relying on.

The post is rich in context: the user implicitly knows the original game well enough to notice the audio change, is actively looking for systematic strategies rather than a single tip, and wants to understand how to leverage music or visual sightlines as exploration guides. This is a beginner-friendly but platform-specific question (remaster behavior vs. original).

An unblocking response would need to cover three things: a navigation walkthrough or checklist for the Drowned Market's key items (particularly those that unlock doors and are commonly missed), clarification on whether the audio cue change was intentional or a remaster bug, and any alternative visual indicators that players can use in lieu of the muted music shift. A short annotated map reference would eliminate the disorientation problem entirely.

### Drill-down: topic 21 — Softlock bug (Bug Reports)

The bug involves a persistent softlock in the remastered game on the Amiga platform. The reproduction window is specific: interacting with the pirate-hopeful protagonist after completing the "Gather the Compass" quest but before starting the "Set Sail" mission. Triggering dialogue with that character at that story point sends the game into an endless conversation loop or a hard freeze after dialogue ends — with music continuing but no player input registered, requiring a full quit-and-reload.

Community speculation in the post points to event-flag corruption: the game appears to fail to update its internal state when the dialogue concludes, leaving it in a hung handoff between quest stages. Some responders noted possible inventory triggers (having specific items present when the interaction fires). Workarounds mentioned include delaying that interaction until after "Set Sail" starts, or clearing inventory to essential quest items first.

What the report lacks for a maintainer to act: no event-flag dump or save-file data; no precise inventory state that reliably triggers vs. avoids the bug; no version string for which remaster build is affected; no confirmation of whether the issue is platform-specific (Amiga only) or also reproducible on other targets. The maintainer needs a reproducible save state and the exact quest-flag values at freeze time.

### Drill-down: topic 23 — Untranslated music stems (Translations & Localization)

Crown of Brine II: The Phantom Galleon contains vocal and text-containing music stems — described as "deep-sea ballads" — that remain untranslated in the remaster. The issue is most pronounced in less prominent areas of the game. Because these musical segments carry embedded story details and lore clues (historical and cultural context about the game world), non-native speakers miss narrative content that is delivered aurally rather than through on-screen text. The scope is broader than a subtitle gap: the untranslated content is part of the environmental storytelling layer.

The user community is requesting either an official patch providing full translations or subtitles for these stems, or a community/modding resource that could serve the same purpose. There is secondary interest in background lore notes explaining the origin and meaning of the songs. Who needs to respond: the game developers own the master stems and could ship a subtitle or lyric sheet patch; failing that, a modder with access to the audio files and translation capability could produce a fan patch. The topic has sat unanswered since September 2025 — the response gap is now over 7 months.

## Recommendations

1. **Triage the three actionable unanswered topics (20, 21, 23) immediately.** All three are 7–9 months old with zero engagement. Topic 21 (softlock) needs a developer acknowledgment and a request for save-state data; topic 23 (music stems) needs either a translation team response or a public localization call-to-arms; topic 20 (Drowned Market navigation) needs a community walkthrough or a moderator nudge to experienced players.

2. **Reduce concentration risk on scurvy_harpooner and salty_cabin-boy.** These two users jointly carry 4 of 7 content categories at CONCENTRATED levels. Cultivate soggy_rigger (10 topics helped, broad participation across 11 threads) as a third anchor. Consider a community recognition mechanism (badges, pinned thank-you post) to signal that helping is valued.

3. **Investigate the March 2026 spike.** Seven topics and 28 posts in a single month is 3–4× the typical rate. If it corresponds to a release event, that event is a template for future activity peaks — and a signal that announcements drive engagement.

4. **Address the remaster audio-cue regression documented in topic 20.** Even if it is by design, the community is interpreting the muted music shift near secret paths as a loss of navigation signal. A developer acknowledgment or FAQ entry would close the topic and prevent future duplicates.

5. **Add a localization contribution guide.** Topic 23 reveals that the community wants to contribute translations but lacks a clear channel. A "How to contribute translations" post in the Translations & Localization category would surface the gap and potentially unlock fan-patch work.
